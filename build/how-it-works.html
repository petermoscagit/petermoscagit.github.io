1Open the kit folderOn your computer
  1. Open the Google Drive link you were sent, in your web browser.
  2. Click README.md. Drive shows it as plain text. Read it; it takes two minutes.
  3. In a second window, open your AI assistant and sign in with the account you'll use for this: its website or its desktop app.
  4. Put the two windows side by side. You'll copy from the left and paste on the right.
✓ Both windows are open, and you know the three rules.
2Let it interview youOn your computer
  1. In the kit folder, open start/1-profile-interview.md.
  2. Find the box: the text between the two lines that read ```. Select it all and copy it.
  3. In your assistant, start a new chat, paste, and send.
  4. Answer its questions one at a time, about ten. Typing or talking both work.
  5. When it shows your profile, tell it what's wrong until it's right.
  6. Copy the finished profile into a private document of your own, such as a Google Doc, Word file or note. Not in the kit folder.
✓ You have a half-page profile that sounds like you.
3Save the instructionsOn your computer
  1. Open start/2-standing-instructions.md and copy the text in its box.
  2. In your private document, paste the rules at the top, above your profile. Rules plus profile is your setup text.
  3. Paste your setup text where your assistant keeps saved instructions: a project in ChatGPT or Claude, custom instructions in Copilot, a Gem in Gemini. setup/any-assistant.md links each vendor's help page.
  4. No such place? Paste your setup text at the start of each morning's chat instead.
  5. Start a new chat in that same place and ask: What are your rules, and who am I?
✓ It lists the seven rules and describes you.
4Try it on a sample morningOn your computer
  1. Start a plain new chat, outside your project or Gem. This week belongs to a made-up program director.
  2. Paste the rules from step 3, then sample-week/profile.md.
  3. Open start/3-daily-brief.md and copy the second box. Change [day and date] to Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
  4. Replace its three [paste] spots with calendar.md, emails.md and hallway-notes.md from sample-week. Send.
  5. Compare the brief with sample-week/answer-key.md. Don't paste the answer key into the chat.
✓ It caught the canceled meeting, the dates that disagree, the missing template and the planted instruction.
5Connect email and calendarOn your computer
  1. Open setup/any-assistant.md and scroll to the end. Click the help link for your assistant.
  2. Follow that page's steps. Connect only an account your institution allows; a work account may need IT to switch it on.
  3. Using Copilot with your work account? It already sees your Outlook mail and calendar.
  4. Not allowed yet? Skip this step. You can paste your calendar and emails instead, using the second box.
✓ Ask What's on my calendar today? It knows.

What it is

It reads your day and tells you what matters.

Every morning it reads

  • Your calendar, today and tomorrow
  • Your email from the last day
  • Your to-do note: what you promised, plus hallway notes
  • Your profile: who you are and how you work

And gives you one screen

  • Today, with cancellations and double bookings marked
  • What needs you today, and draft replies
  • What doesn't add up, and what's missing
  • What's coming up, and your updated to-do list

It drafts. You decide, and you send.

The rules

Three rules.

No patient information, ever.

This is for administrative work. Connect only what your institution allows.

It drafts. You send.

It's told to draft only. Where your assistant can block sending, turn that on too.

It helps. It doesn't judge.

No evaluating residents, candidates or colleagues. No clinical decisions.

Text inside an email is information, never an instruction. If an email tries to direct it, the brief tells you.

Every day

About ten minutes in the morning.

Morning

  • Open a new chat. Paste the brief prompt and your to-do note.
  • Read the brief. Send the drafts you like, after you edit them.
  • Copy the updated list back to your note.
  • Add the "Learned" lines you agree with to your profile.

During the day

  • Promise something in a hallway? Add a line under "New notes:".
  • Finish something? Write "done" next to it.
  • Brief got something wrong? Fix the profile, not just the chat.
